Machine Learning and Deep Learning course project, 2025, with Alice Banaudi, Carla Finocchiaro and Alessandra Marchese. ADACS reaches 29.76% mIoU with a ResNet-18 backbone and 37.01% mIoU with ResNet-101, outperforming DACS and the other baselines. PyTorch implementation with training scripts for baseline, domain shift, data augmentation, adversarial adaptation, DACS and ADACS.
