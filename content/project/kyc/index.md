Project for the AI Lab: Computer Vision and NLP course at Sapienza, 2022/2023, with Siria Sannino, Christian Bianchi and Nicola Mastrorilli. It combines bitmask and edge detection for card analysis with a convolutional neural network for classification, packaged with a Tkinter GUI (Keras, OpenCV).
