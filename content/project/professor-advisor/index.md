Human-Computer Interaction course at Sapienza, 2023/2024, with the Intrusi di ACSAI team (Veronica Di Gennaro, Annafabia Gai, Christian Gennarelli, Daniel Y. Guarnizo Orjuela). Full process from needfinding (interviews and questionnaires), storyboarding and paper prototyping to a tested digital prototype covering note sharing, professor reviews and internship discovery.
