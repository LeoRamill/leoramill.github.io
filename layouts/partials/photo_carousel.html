{{/* One photograph held large in the middle with its neighbours peeking in at the sides, advancing on its own. Built on the same `photo_item` the strip uses, so a photograph behaves the same in both: click to open it full size. dict: `photos`, `label`, `height` (thumbnail height, default 720 — a centre slide is large), `interval` (milliseconds between advances, default 7000 — the first advance after the row comes into view is sooner, so that it is seen to move at all), `captions` (optional map of file name to text). The caption belongs to the middle photograph and is written once under the row rather than once per slide: a line on a dimmed neighbour is noise, and three lines at once contradict the whole point of holding one picture in the middle. A photograph with no entry in the map simply has no caption, and the line collapses rather than leaving a gap. Put `data-photo-scope` on the wrapper, as for the strip, so the nav arrows find the track. */}} {{ $label := .label | default "Photos" }} {{ $h := .height | default 720 }} {{ $interval := .interval | default 7000 }} {{ $caps := .captions | default dict }}
